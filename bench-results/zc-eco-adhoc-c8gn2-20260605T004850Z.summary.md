## Takeaways

- Local performance is strong when topology is explicit: 63.5 Gbit/s and 1.94M 4K frames/s on the aligned local WAL fan.
- Cheap EC2 `c8gn.2xlarge` gets about 40 Gbit/s raw topology-aligned TCP between client and fan.
- Best EC2 WAL fan result was 17.7 Gbit/s, about 540k 4K write frames/s.
- Adaptive wait is useful but modest: it cut fan voluntary context switches versus blocking and gained about 2%.
- Greedy wait did not buy enough to justify it as a default.
- Larger WAL batches/windows currently regress; the fan result/ACK path needs more work before bigger coalesced chunks pay off.
- The tools correctly warned on a mismatched write window for batch 128.
