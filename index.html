Identity laboratories

These shared debug integrations exercise issuer boundaries, scoped permissions, supplementary approvals and revocation. The storage operating procedures belong to zccusan day-two operations.

  • SAML and real-time revocation: an OAuth-backed IdP and two nginx auth proxies.
  • Federated identity laboratory: independent OAuth issuers and time-limited approval.

The laboratories describe their local evidence and production limitations. Local exercises create no AWS resources; opt-in real AWS checks have separate cost and teardown instructions.
